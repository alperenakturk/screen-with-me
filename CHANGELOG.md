# Changelog

What changed for people using Sidelit, newest first. The website and the extension share one version number (shown
in the site's footer and on the extension page). Every version that went live after its tests passed is tagged in
this repository as `v<version>` (from 0.9.97.70 on).

Versions count in four parts before 1.0: the fourth part is a release, the third part moves for a bigger step.

## 0.9.97 — 3 to 8 November 2026

The step before 0.9.98: a door the host can open or close, private messages, two names in one browser told apart,
the Test Lab (fake friends, scenarios, network tricks, locked behind the owner's code), the terms of use and a privacy
policy rewritten for KVKK and GDPR, an uptime check, release tags and this changelog, four rounds of security fixes,
cameras that behave on phones, a lighter and faster site (only your language and only the page's styles are
downloaded), and many new games: the Classics table (tic-tac-toe, Four in a Row, Turkish dama, chess, backgammon,
Reversi) with Glowbeam as a referee and opponent, Sea Battle, a bigger quiz and Draw & Guess with topics, and the side
games' tip jar, cuttings and badges.

### 0.9.97.106 — 10 Nov
- Stub, the cinema ticket, is now the games' mascot: the referee who cheers and gasps, the bot you play against in the
  Classics and Sea Battle, the cashier at the Snack Bar and the usher whose torch lights your window garden. He cheers
  on the result card when you win and looks a little sorry when you lose.
- Every table in the games hall wears its own drawn badge, the same one you see on its lobby card.
- A little motion: the game window slides in as it opens, chess and dama pieces slide to their new square, and Last
  Heart's cards flip over when they turn face up. If your device is set to reduce motion, none of it plays.

### 0.9.97.104 — 10 Nov
- Snack Bar: every place has its own shop-front colours; a seasonal stand comes each week (hot cocoa, lemonade, cotton
  candy, caramel apples) and earns its own badge at level 5; after each film the receipt has a short manager's report
  (what sold most and what to buy next); each stand plays its own little sound when it reaches a milestone. Your saved
  Snack Bar carries over.

### 0.9.97.103 — 10 Nov
- Five Frames Duel: pick "Best of three" in the lobby for a short match (the first game is the word of the day, then
  practice words, with the score between games), and "Hard mode", where every letter you found must be used again.
  The Duel has its own cover in the games hall.
- Sea Battle on a phone: the first tap zooms into that corner of their sea so the cells are easy to hit, the second tap
  fires; "Whole sea" goes back at any time.

### 0.9.97.102 — 10 Nov
- A new theme, **Matinee**: the first light theme, warm paper with ink-dark text and a rust accent, for watching in
  daylight. The video's own place stays black.
- The Ocean Liner theme is gone. If you had picked it, you now see Night; nothing else changes.
- The home page now takes its colours from your theme (the slogan, the main button, the steps and the little chat bubble
  in the picture), like the room already did. Night keeps the gold of the logo.
- Sunset's glow moved to the right, so "How it works" on the home page is easy to read again.
- New default font: Sidelit (Bricolage Grotesque titles with Figtree text). If you prefer your device's own font, pick
  it in ⚙️ → My screen → Font.
- On a computer or a tablet, the camera window now starts at the bottom left of the video, like Meet, just above the
  video's own controls. Moving it keeps your place as before; double-clicking its bar brings it back there.

### 0.9.97.101 — 10 Nov
- Games on a phone held sideways: chess, dama, backgammon, Reversi and the others show the board at the full height
  with the players and buttons beside it; Sea Battle shows one sea at a time, big; Five Frames, the Five Frames Duel,
  Snack Bar and Window Garden lay out in two columns; the Classics shelf fits in one row. Nothing is cut off or shrunk
  to tiny text any more.
- Every table with a lobby (Quiz, Draw & Guess, Last Heart, the Five Frames Duel) opens with the same card: the game's
  badge, a short rule line, who is here and the settings.
- In a game window's head, "in the video's place" and full screen now have clearly different icons and clearer
  tooltips.

### 0.9.97.100 — 10 Nov
- A new "Font" setting (⚙️ → My screen): keep your device's own font or pick one of six: Sidelit, Poster, Polished,
  Readable, Rounded or Easy reading. Only the font you pick is downloaded, and every Turkish letter is in each.
- The home page's big title now really uses the logo's typeface (it fell back to the device's font before).

### 0.9.97.99 — 10 Nov
- Games: new table, ⚔️ Five Frames Duel. Race a friend to the same five-letter word, in English or Turkish: you see
  their marks as they guess, never their letters, and the first to find the word wins. The first game is the word of
  the day, "Play again" brings a practice word. If neither finds it in seven tries, it is a draw.

### 0.9.97.98 — 10 Nov
- Easier to read in every theme: on the picture themes the home page's text now sits on a calm, darker area, so the
  picture behind it never hides a word (the Ocean Liner theme's light sky was the worst).
- Text boxes, the search box and switches have clearly visible edges in every theme.
- The plain themes are calmer: the drifting colour clouds and the corner glows are gone.
- Night theme: your own chat messages and the games hall's counts are easier to read.

### 0.9.97.97 — 10 Nov
- Games: Snack Bar grows into a proper snack-bar game. Eight stands (new: a hot dog grill, an ice cream cart and an
  arcade), each with its own price, recipes that double a stand, a menu bonus for keeping many stands busy, four staff
  to hire (one serves customers for you), a "special of the day" and two new places (by the sea, a planetarium). Buy
  ×1, ×10 or as many as you can afford. Your saved Snack Bar comes along: levels, coins and decorations stay, and your
  stars are converted so their bonus never drops.
- Games: a fresh look for the counter, the new stands and more kinds of customers.

### 0.9.97.96 — 10 Nov
- Phones: every game fills the whole screen when the phone is held sideways too (on big phones it used to open as a
  small window over the video).
- Phones: your own screen-share preview starts small in the top corner of the video, so the video's play and time bar
  stays free.
- Big screens: the side games (Snack Bar, Window Garden, Five Frames) open larger on big monitors, and dialogs such as
  the invite window grow a little on very wide screens.

### 0.9.97.95 — 9 Nov
- On networks where the room uses its backup connection (some phone hotspots), each tab now asks the server about half
  as often, and one network can keep up to 12 such connections open across all rooms.
- When something goes wrong on the server, the page gets a short, clear answer instead of an error page, and the server
  tries once more by itself where that is safe.

### 0.9.97.94 — 9 Nov
- Games: holding down R in Sea Battle turns the ship once, and holding Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y in Draw & Guess undoes or redoes
  once, instead of repeating so fast that the game could lose its connection.

### 0.9.97.93 — 9 Nov
- The site uses much less of its free server allowance, so a busy evening (or one person misusing it) can no longer
  stop every room for the rest of the day. A room where people only watch now costs about a sixth of what it did.
- A page that sends far too much at once is reconnected with a short note, and one network can hold up to 12
  connections in a room (enough for a family or a phone carrier sharing one address).
- Chat images are kept for a day (a week in a saved room); after that the message stays and shows "Image".
- Behind the scenes: lower daily totals for uploaded images, and a 30-day cap on what all rooms can store.

### 0.9.97.92 — 8 Nov
- Behind the scenes: the site now checks its own health every 15 minutes from inside its server and tells the team
  when it is down. Nothing changes for people using it.

### 0.9.97.91 — 8 Nov
- Games: after a reload, a Classics table says "Setting up the table…" until it is back, instead of flashing an empty
  tic-tac-toe grid for a moment.

### 0.9.97.90 — 8 Nov
- Behind the scenes only: the games' test tools keep working after a table has been quiet for a while, so our
  automatic tests no longer fail at random. Nothing changes on the live site.

### 0.9.97.89 — 8 Nov
- A room no longer turns into the browser's "no internet" page when your Wi-Fi stays connected but the internet drops
  (a weak phone hotspot, say). The page waits, checks every 10 seconds, and once the site answers again it reloads
  once, straight back into the room.

### 0.9.97.88 — 8 Nov

- Rooms: a short Wi-Fi drop no longer throws you out to the browser's "no internet" page; at worst the room reloads
  once by itself when the connection is back.

### 0.9.97.87 — 8 Nov
- A phone held sideways no longer offers "Watch fullscreen" next to a video that can't be played there.

### 0.9.97.86 — 8 Nov
- The "a new version of the extension is out" bubble no longer lands on top of the first-time tips in a new room (it
  waits for them), and no longer points at nothing on a phone or a narrow window.
- Shorts on a phone: a "waiting…" note and a friend's message no longer share the bar's slot at once; the note goes
  first.
- Test Lab: its 🐞 button no longer covers the Shorts notes, the phone's 😀 or the Shorts bar.

### 0.9.97.85 — 8 Nov

  same picture cards for "How to play". Glowbeam now cheers or gasps in a corner of the Quiz, Draw & Guess and Last

  never about someone's mistake.

### 0.9.97.84 — 8 Nov

  empty corner left of the video on a computer and in the bar under the video on a phone.

### 0.9.97.83 — 8 Nov
- Faster first page: the home page and the connection test no longer download the room's styles, which load only

### 0.9.97.82 — 8 Nov

### 0.9.97.81 — 8 Nov

### 0.9.97.80 — 8 Nov
