- Quiz **Tournament**: the host picks Tournament and 1 or 2 picks each. Each round is 3 questions on a topic that the
  players choose in turn; the best score in a round wins it, and the most round wins takes the tournament (a tie goes
  to points). The podium shows round wins and points.
- Two new **jokers** beside 50:50, each once a game and one per question: **×2 Double** (a right answer scores
  double) and **↺ Second chance** (a wrong answer may be tried once more, for half points). On a typed question 50:50
  shows the first letter and the length.
