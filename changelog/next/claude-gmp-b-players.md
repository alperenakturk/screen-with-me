- Sea Battle for 2 to 6 players: on your turn pick whose sea to attack, then fire. A fleet that sinks is out but keeps
  watching; the last fleet afloat wins, and the result lists every place.
- Five Frames Duel for 2 to 6 players: everyone races on the same word, the race goes on until every board is done,
  and the result lists the places in finishing order.
