- Push-to-talk works with any key you choose: press the key button next to "Push to talk" and then the key you want.
  The room's own shortcuts and key combinations are refused, and Esc cancels.
- The chosen key also works on the film page with the browser extension.
- A new "🪟 Talk window" keeps a small hold-to-talk button on top of your other tabs and windows, so you can talk to
  your friends while another tab is in front (Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera on a computer).
