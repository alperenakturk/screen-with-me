the cloud session unless said (`blog.kosmi.io` failed with ENOTFOUND). **WEAK** = single / vendor / SEO source,
**GUESS** = inference. Related: overview.md._

## Product and platforms
- Launched publicly on **15 March 2020**, the first lockdown weekend, as a side project that took off; reached #1 on
  Hacker News (co-founder quote, Comcast LIFT article) [7]. Co-founders: Haukur Rosinkranz (CEO) and Jim Rand (COO,
  Reykjavik) [7]. App Store developer: **Kosmi Inc.** [4]. A Spanish startup registry lists Toronto HQ, 2–10 staff and
  ~€209 K raised (WEAK, conflicts with the Reykjavik listing) [14].
- **Comcast NBCUniversal LIFT Labs Accelerator (Techstars), class of 2022**; Demo Day 2 Nov 2022; Kosmi said it had
  grown to ~200 K monthly users and planned a paid tier and a later 3D / VR version [7][15].
- Rooms are themed spaces: "virtual living room, drive-in, games room, coffee shop, pub" (vendor copy, WEAK) [16].
- Pitch: "no sign-up, no downloads", everything in the browser; plus native mobile apps [1][2].

## Supported services and how
- **Synced players:** YouTube and local video files (vendor) [2]. AlternativeTo also lists synced YouTube and video
  co-watching [17].
- **Shared virtual browser:** a browser window "everyone in the room can see, click and type in" (vendor, on its
  Hyperbeam-alternative page) [12]. The video is rendered once (host machine or a server) and streamed to guests, so
  sync is by construction, not by commanding each person's player (GUESS from the product shape).
- **DRM services:** in 2020 SCMP described Netflix in Kosmi as the host **sharing their screen**; only the host needs a
  subscription [8]. Kosmi's SEO pages (Disney+ / Max watch party) promise these services "in perfect sync" [9][18].
  Browser DRM normally blacks out captured video, so these paths are fragile (GUESS; no direct test found). This is the
  opposite of Sidelit's rule (each person's own subscription, DRM never bypassed).
- **Screen share:** free up to **720p**, 1080p is Premium (vendor) [12][13].

## Room features
- Voice, video and text chat in one room; screen share; shared browser; synced video; games [1][7][12].
- **Games / apps:** poker and retro console emulators named in press [7]; an AlternativeTo user wishes emulation had
  "no lag for non-host players" [17], which suggests the emulator runs on the host and streams to others (GUESS).
- Casting to a TV is listed as a Premium perk ("unlimited casting"), so a free tier with a cap exists (WEAK) [10].
- Room size limits: not found. Premium "bigger rooms" is mentioned in older research, without numbers.
- Moderation tools (kick / ban): not found in excerpts.

## Onboarding and accounts
- **Host:** open kosmi.io → create a room → copy the invite link. A Nov 2026 third-party test says creating a room
  asked them to sign in first (WEAK, from a competitor's blog) [10]; Kosmi's own pages say rooms are free with no
  sign-up [12][19].
- **Guest:** open the link in a browser or app; joining without an account [10][19].
- **Netflix-type nights:** the host opens the service in screen share or the shared browser; guests need nothing [8].

## Phone experience
- Native apps on iOS (iOS 15+ on the US store; other storefronts say 14+) and Android [4][6].
- App Store excerpts: a recent update makes "the chat box cut off subtitles" in landscape; screen sharing "unusable on
  mobile and in-app" [4][5].
- Kosmi actively targets phone users who lost Rave on iOS (Rave removed from the App Store, Aug 2025) with a "Rave
  alternative" page and blog post [13][20].

## Pricing, limits, ads and income
- **Free:** rooms, shared browser, video chat, games, screen share up to 720p (vendor) [12].
- **Premium:** $7.99 / month or $59.99 / year per a Nov 2026 competitor review (WEAK) [10]; older Kosmi blog feed says
  $4.99 / month (WEAK, undated) [11]. Perks named: 1080p (and 4K per one source) screen share, **unlimited time in the
  shared browser** (so the free shared browser is time-limited), unlimited TV casting, themes, a members-only lounge
  [10][12].
- **Upsell in the room:** a July 2026 Google Play review says a Premium prompt appeared mid-movie and would not close
  (via Glint, WEAK) [10].
- No ads found. Income = Premium subscriptions (GUESS: plus Comcast partnership work in 2022 [7]).
- Growth engine: SEO "X alternative" pages for every rival and every dead tool: Kast, Rave, Hyperbeam, Teleparty,
  Discord, Metastream, Scener, TwoSeven, Netflix Party, plus "Disney+ / Max watch party" and "screen share with
  friends" pages, several retitled "2026" [9][12][13][18][19][21].

## Privacy
- App Store privacy label (Croatian storefront excerpt): **Data Linked to You** may include contact info, user content,
  identifiers and usage data; a "Data Not Linked to You" section exists (contents not captured) [5].
- Privacy policy itself: not retrieved. Account deletion process: not found.
- The shared browser runs logins (e.g. a host's streaming account) inside a third-party environment; send.win notes
  this risk for virtual browsers in general (WEAK) [22].

Complaint themes:
1. **Premium upsell interrupting a film** (prompt mid-movie that will not close) [10].
2. **Phone UI:** chat box covers subtitles in landscape; mobile screen share unusable [4].
3. **Lag for non-hosts** in streamed games / emulators [17].
4. **Sign-in to host** despite "no sign-up" marketing (WEAK) [10].
5. Older research also logged "can't cancel Premium" (WEAK, `market-and-competitors-2026.md`); not re-confirmed.
6. SaaSHub comment: "many people are planning to leave Kosmi", no reason given (WEAK) [17].

- Android app updated 21 Aug 2026; version 2.1.21 dated Apr 2026 by APKCombo (WEAK) [6][24].
- Glint says Kosmi was running in Oct 2026 with no status-page outages since Aug 2026 (WEAK) [10].

## Against Sidelit
- Where they are ahead:
  - **Native iOS and Android apps** and an installed base (~200 K MAU in 2022) [7].
  - **Shared virtual browser**: any site, including ones our extension cannot reach, with nothing installed.
  - **Phone guests on any service** (they watch a stream, not their own player).
  - **Emulators / poker** and themed room spaces; TV casting.
  - Years of **SEO pages** that rank for every rival's name.
- Where Sidelit is ahead:
  - **No paywall and no upsell in the room**; free 720p limit and time-limited shared browser vs our free everything.
  - **Native-quality picture:** each person plays the real video at full resolution; no 720p screen-share stream,
    no lag for non-hosts.
  - **Clean on DRM:** we never relay a DRM stream; Kosmi's Netflix path is screen share of the host's account.
  - **Sync engine** with ready-wait and "3-2-1 together"; Shorts / TikTok "scroll together"; saved rooms, pins, votes,
    recap; HTTP fallback.
  - **Phone UI rules** (no view covers the film; no scroll inside views) vs "chat box covers subtitles" complaints.
  - **Privacy:** no account, no store-tracked identifiers.

## Ideas worth taking
1. **"Alternative" landing pages, done honestly.** Kosmi's main growth tool. A few English pages ("watch YouTube
   together, no sign-up", "after Rave left iPhone: watch together in the browser") that say what Sidelit does, never
   disparaging, never naming piracy sites. Why: people search for a dead tool's name. Effort M. Fit: lane A + Research
   copy; after the own domain (SEO on `workers.dev` is weak, GUESS).
2. **Themed room "spaces"** (living room, drive-in, café) as theme presets with a matching scene. Why: Kosmi sells the
   feeling of a place; we already have illustrated themes. Effort S–M. Fit: Design + lane A.
3. **Landscape phone check for subtitles:** a Test Lab scenario that fails if chat or the rail overlaps the bottom 15%
   of the video in `html.sideways`. Why: Kosmi's top app complaint. Effort S. Fit: lane A.
4. **Never interrupt a film with a prompt.** Write it as a rule for the coming donation flow (only on the home page
   or after the recap). Why: Kosmi's mid-movie Premium popup is a 2026 complaint. Effort S. Fit: Planner rule, after 1.0.
5. **A "Rave on iPhone is gone" answer:** a help entry for phone users ("add Sidelit to the home screen; YouTube, Shorts,
   TikTok, files work"). Effort S. Fit: lane A.
6. **Retro-style table game** (a simple shared arcade, our own, server-authoritative so no host lag). Why: Kosmi's
   emulators draw hangout crowds; ours would avoid the non-host lag complaint. Effort L. Fit: Games, after 1.0.
7. Shared virtual browser: **not recommended** (server cost, DRM relay risk, against our "each person's own player"
   rule). Listed only to record the decision.
