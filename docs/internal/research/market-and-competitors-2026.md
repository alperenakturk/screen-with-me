# Market and competitors 2026 (watch together)

_Research session, 3 Nov 2026, Job 8 from the Planner. Replaces the older Turkish analysis
[`../rakip-analizi.md`](../rakip-analizi.md) (17 Oct 2026, kept for history). Method: web search only (store pages,
Reddit, analyst sites and most vendor pages could not be opened from the cloud session), so every number is as a
search excerpt gave it; **WEAK** = vendor / SEO / single source. Not investment or legal advice._

## Headline
The platforms have left the field (Disney+ GroupWatch ended Sep 2023, Prime Video Watch Party Apr 2024), SharePlay
is Apple-only and without Netflix, Discord's Watch Together is YouTube-only with ads, and the old open tools are dead
(Metastream on MV2). The leaders that remain charge money (Teleparty, Kosmi, Scener), show ads (Watch2Gether) or
fight app stores (Rave). **The open space is ours on paper: free, no account, no ads, any HTML5 site + YouTube /
Shorts / TikTok, with voice, cameras, screen share and games, on computer and phone.** The hard part is not the
product; it is being found.

## 1. Market

### Size and usage
- **One analyst figure, weak:** "watch party platforms" $2.84 B in 2025 → $8.92 B by 2034 (13.8 % CAGR), North
  America 38 %, Europe 29 % (Dataintelo, internally inconsistent; no second report found)
  ([Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/watch-party-platforms-market), WEAK).
- **Pandemic peak, US:** ~1 in 7 adult SVOD users, ≈ 25 M adults, joined an online watch party (TDG survey, 2,000
  SVOD users, late 2020 – early 2021) ([VideoNuze](https://www.videonuze.com/article/streaming-watch-parties-attract-25-million-u-s-adults-during-pandemic));
  23 % used a co-viewing app in 2021, Amazon Watch Party the most used (44 %) (Hub, non-scientific)
  ([NextTV](https://www.nexttv.com/news/co-viewing-app-usage-surges-has-tvs-social-revolution-finally-arrived)).
- **Today:** 21 % of US adults text loved ones while watching the same film remotely (Tubi / Harris "The Stream
  2024", 2,503 adults) ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2024/03/23/streaming-can-foster-shared-moments-or-be-precious-alone-time)).
  48 % of all viewing time is spent with others, mostly in the same room (MRI 2019)
  ([NielsenIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2019/half-of-all-video-viewing-time-is-spent-with-others-mri/)).
- **Biggest single products:** Teleparty claims 10 M users (Chrome Web Store 10,000,000+, 4.1★ / ~17.3 K ratings,
  Jun 2026) ([CWS](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oocalimimngaihdkbihfgmpkcpnmlaoa)); Rave 25 M+ downloads
  (aggregator, WEAK); Discord > 200 M monthly users with no Watch Together figure
  ([Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2580923/discord-launches-first-checkpoint-year-end-recap)).

**Reading:** a real but modest, post-pandemic market; demand is steady (people text each other while watching apart),
supply has thinned out.

### Who the users are
- **Young:** 41 % of 16–34-year-olds have co-watched remotely vs 23 % of 35–54 and 3 % of 55+ (undated, WEAK)
  ([StreamTV Insider](https://www.streamtvinsider.com/video/co-viewing-gaining-popularity-but-mostly-younger-viewers)).
  Gen Z co-views social feeds together (YPulse, 28 Sep 2026, paywalled)
  ([YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/article/2026/09/28/ypulse-explains-gen-zs-social-media-coviewing/)).
- **Use cases with evidence:** Discord communities (watching inside voice channels), families / couples (GroupWatch was
  "friends and family"), short video shared between friends (Instagram Blend, Apr 2025:
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/17/instagrams-new-blend-feature-creates-a-custom-reels-feed-for-you-and-your-friends/)).
  Long-distance couples and study groups are the classic story but no hard data was found.
- **Regions:** North America first, Europe second (WEAK). No Turkey data.

### Trends 2023–2026
1. **Streamers removed their own watch parties:** Disney+ GroupWatch (18 Sep 2023,
   [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/streaming/disney-plus-just-removed-one-of-its-best-friends-and-family-focused-features)),
   Prime Video Watch Party + Twitch's version (2 Apr 2024, "low usage",
   [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/twitch-is-ending-its-pandemic-era-prime-video-watch-parties-110004438.html)),
   Plex dropping Watch Together from new apps
   ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2619590/plex-is-dropping-a-popular-feature-from-its-new-streaming-apps.html));
   Hulu's status unknown. Netflix never had one.
2. **OS / platform features:** SharePlay (Apple only, Netflix and Prime absent); Discord Activities opened to all
   developers in Sep 2024 ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/discord-opens-activities-in-app-games-and-features-to-all-developers/)).
   YouTube's own co-watch exists only on Meta Quest (beta) and via Google Meet with YouTube Premium.
3. **Short video together:** Instagram Blend (shared Reels feed, not synced), small tools for TikTok / Shorts
   (TandemParty, SofaScroll), see `newcomers-help-shorts.md` §4.
4. **App-store risk for social rooms:** Rave removed from the App Store in Aug 2025 (moderation / content complaints),
   suing Apple in five countries since May 2026
   ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/05/07/shareplay-is-not-why-co-streaming-app-rave-was-kicked-off-the-app-store)).
5. **SEO war among survivors:** Kosmi publishes "X alternative" pages for every dead or troubled rival
   ([kosmi.io/kast-alternative](https://kosmi.io/kast-alternative/)).
6. **AI:** no evidence it matters in this category yet.

## 4. How they grew, and our honest chances

### How each grew
- **Teleparty:** launched 2016 as Netflix Party, a niche tool for couples; the March 2020 lockdowns made it explode
  (USA Today, Business Insider, LA Times coverage); the exact-match name "Netflix Party" topped Chrome Web Store search
  (inference). ([KnowYourMeme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/netflix-party))
- **Watch2Gether:** ~2011, Berlin; steady SEO on "watch YouTube together" and word of mouth (inference).
- **Kosmi:** pandemic press (2020) + years of "X alternative" SEO pages for every dying rival.
- **Rave:** first mobile watch-party app (from WeMesh), app-store discovery, public rooms with strangers (which later
  cost it iOS).
- **Scener:** HBO partnership (May 2020) + big hosted events (Comic-Con, RiffTrax); spikes did not last.
- **Hyperbeam:** university project → YC W22 → pivot to an API when the consumer app plateaued.
- **Discord:** stepped in when YouTube killed the music bots (Sep 2021); distribution = Discord itself.
- **Metastream:** Product Hunt / Reddit, open source; died of maintenance (DRM licence, MV3).

### Where we can win
1. **"Free, no account, no ads" as the headline**: the exact pain points of the leaders (Teleparty billing, W2G ads).
2. **Shorts / TikTok together on phones, no install**: nobody else does it well; matches Gen Z co-viewing.
3. **Any HTML5 site with a clean extension** (legal: no re-streaming), where Teleparty's list stops.
4. **Turkey first as a beachhead**: Turkish UI already, the owner's own network, little local competition.
5. **Small friend groups with voice + games**: a "hangout" like Kosmi but free.

### Where we can't (now)
- Netflix / Disney+ / Prime parties: Teleparty owns that, and adapters are after 1.0.
- Big public communities: Discord owns them; public rooms bring moderation and store risks (Rave).
- Paid acquisition, studio deals, press at Teleparty's scale.

### The 3 biggest risks
1. **Discovery:** zero installed base, a new name, and a crowded Chrome Web Store search; the product could be great
   and still unseen.
2. **Platform dependence:** YouTube's API terms and quota (audit pending), Chrome Web Store review of an "all sites"
   extension, TikTok's embed player: each can break a core feature from outside.
3. **One-person maintenance:** sites change players, browsers change rules (the Metastream fate); the owner is a
   student with no budget, so scope must stay small after 1.0.

### 5 growth moves for the first 3 months after 1.0 (solo, no budget)
1. **Own the store listing and its words.** Chrome Web Store title / short description built on what people search
   ("watch together", "watch party", "YouTube together", "sync any video"), the "no account, no ads" promise, 5 good
   screenshots and a promo video (`hyperframes` / `video-use` in the owner's catalogue); ask the first 20 users for a
   review in week 1.
2. **SEO pages that answer the searches the dying rivals leave behind**, honestly: "Watch YouTube Shorts together",
   "Watch2Gether without ads", "Teleparty alternative, free, no account", "Watch TikTok with friends", "Metastream
   alternative" (Kosmi's playbook, without naming piracy sites). English first, Turkish second.
3. **Short-video content that shows the product**: 15–30 s clips of "scrolling Shorts together with my friend in
   another city", the 3-2-1 start, Draw & Guess in a room; posted on TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts weekly.
4. **Communities where the need is**: answer threads in r/LongDistance, r/discordapp, r/Chromebook, student Discords
   and Turkish forums (Ekşi Sözlük, Technopat) with a plain "I built this, free, no account" post; a Product Hunt
   launch day with friends lined up.
5. **Make every room a referral**: the invite card (QR, WhatsApp, "I saved you a seat 🍿"), the end-of-session recap
   image to share, and a one-click "Add to Chrome" on the "Go to the film" card once the store listing exists.
