# Name ideas (Oct 2026)

> Inspiration for the final product name (ROADMAP §4, "Final product name"). **Nothing is decided and nothing in the
> code is renamed**: the decision belongs to the owner. Earlier trademark research: `name-research.md`.
> Visual version (Turkish UI, wordmark mocks, favorites): the "Yeni Ad Fikirleri" artifact
> (https://claude.ai/artifact/PQ5MoBZgkxQrqtasNmtn3q, private to the owner).

**Owner's direction (round 2):** we are a global product, so the name is **English first**; Turkish and other
languages come after (see CLAUDE.md "Language priority"). Round 1 leaned Turkish and is kept below for reference.

**Method and limits:** web search only (28 Oct 2026); finalists were re-checked with a deeper search after the owner found "Same Scene" online. Domain lookups (RDAP/whois) were blocked by the session's
network, so **no domain was checked**. "clean" means "no same-name product found by search engines", not "free to
register". Before deciding, run the checks in `name-research.md` §2 (TÜRKPATENT, TMview, USPTO, WIPO, Chrome Web
Store, domains) for the 2–3 finalists.

**General findings:** avoid popcorn names ("Popcorn Time" is a famous piracy app); avoid sync / couch / watch /
party / tele- (crowded); many short cinema words (Marquee, Spool) are already used by streaming or video apps.

## Round 2: English-first

### Top 5

| # | Name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Armrest** | Clean in both quick and deep search. A vivid everyday image nobody in our category uses, a great mascot, short. A common word, but not descriptive, so it can be a trademark. |
| 2 | **Sofaloop** | Clean in every search. Invented, so the safest choice for trademark and domains. |
| 3 | **Nightseat** | Clean in both searches. "Your seat for movie night", fits our night themes and the saved-seat invite. |
| 4 | **Twinseat** | Instantly understood and easy to spell. Caution: an airline sells a "Twin-Seat" (different field). |
| 5 | **Glowroom** | Cozy and modern, a glowing logo. Caution: an iOS photo editor uses the name. |

### All candidates by direction

Status key: `clean` = no same-name product found · `caution` = same name used elsewhere · `taken` = taken in or near our category · `risky` = risky meaning or confusion · `unchecked` = not checked yet.

#### The seat next to you

English version of the invite card idea ("I saved you a seat"): the small physical detail of sitting next to a friend.

| Name | Meaning | Feel | Say it (EN / TR ear) | Sample tagline | Quick check | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Armrest** | The shared armrest between two cinema seats: the most "next to you" object there is. | Concrete, warm, a little funny. A strong, simple mascot (two elbows on one armrest). | ARM-rest / arm-rest ("arm" kol, "rest" dayanak) | "Share the armrest, from anywhere." | `clean` No app, extension or startup named Armrest found (quick and deep search). | A common English word: the .com is surely taken, a word mark is still possible because it does not describe the service. |
| **Twinseat** | Two seats side by side, like a love seat at the cinema. | Clear, friendly, easy to spell after hearing it once. | TWIN-seat / tvin-sit | "Two seats, one screen." | `caution` Air New Zealand sells a "Twin-Seat" (two economy seats for one person); Twinset is a fashion brand. | An airline product name and a fashion brand nearby; "Twin" also suggests exactly two people. |
| **Nextseat** | "I saved you the next seat." | Inviting, reads as an action. | NEXT-seat / nekst-sit | "I saved you the next seat." | `caution` A 2014 student app (Pepperdine classmates) used NextSeat; seems inactive. Also "nexSeat", a restaurant app. | Old namesakes; "next" can read as "next video". |
| **Elbowroom** | "Elbow room": space to be comfortable, together. | Relaxed, a bit witty. | EL-bo-room / elbou-rum | "Plenty of elbow room for everyone." | `clean` No app; a Dublin yoga studio and some pubs use the name. | Long (9 letters); "room" is close to our generic "room" word. |
| **Seatmate** | The person in the seat next to you. | Friendly, familiar. | SEAT-mate / sit-meyt | "Find your seatmate." | `caution` SeatMate is a Korean library seat-booking app; the word is common in airlines. | "-mate" names are crowded in our category (WatchMates). |
| **Plus One** | Bringing a friend along ("you're my plus one"). | Social, playful, short. | plus-WUN / plas-van | "Every movie needs a plus one." | `caution` "Plus 1" is an active social plans app (joinplus1.com). | Common phrase, existing social app; also Google+ memories. |

#### Same scene, same beat

English versions of "Ayrı ekranlar, aynı an.": everyone on the same frame at the same time.

| Name | Meaning | Feel | Say it (EN / TR ear) | Sample tagline | Quick check | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Samescene** | Everyone on the same scene. The English twin of "Ayrı ekranlar, aynı an." | Rhymes with itself, explains sync without saying "sync". | SAME-seen / seym-sin | "Different screens. Same scene." | `taken` Taken: "SameScene: Movie & TV Tracker" on Google Play matches people watching the same title and episode (found in a deeper search; the first quick search missed it). | Same field (social viewing). Drop it; the tagline "Different screens. Same scene." can still be used as a slogan. |
| **Lockstep** | Moving in lockstep: perfectly in sync. | Precise, confident, a bit serious. | LOCK-step / lok-step | "Your friends, in lockstep." | `caution` Lockstep is a Mac timecode utility (Figure 53) that syncs video, and an Obsidian sync plugin. | Military/marching feel; a video-sync tool already uses the name. |
| **Unison** | In unison: together at the same moment. | Elegant, musical. | YOO-ni-sun / yu-ni-sın | "Watch in unison." | `caution` Not searched in detail; Unison is a well-known file-sync program and a UK trade union. | Very common name, hard to own. |

Blanket, glow, night: the feeling of a movie night at home, without the crowded "couch" words.

| Name | Meaning | Feel | Say it (EN / TR ear) | Sample tagline | Quick check | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nightseat** | Your seat for movie night. | Evening, calm, matches the "gece" theme. | NITE-seat / nayt-sit | "Movie night, saved seat." | `clean` No product named Nightseat found (quick and deep search; "Nightset" was a London nightlife marketplace). | Sounds night-only; we are also for afternoon Shorts and games. |
| **Glowroom** | A room lit only by the screen's glow. | Cozy, modern, a nice glowing logo. | GLOW-room / glou-rum | "One glow, many rooms." | `caution` Glowroom is an iOS HDR photo editor; also beauty lounges and shops named Glow Room. | A photo app (software) already uses it; strong beauty-salon feel. |
| **Snug** | Snug: warm, close, comfortable. | Tiny (4 letters), soft, huggable. | SNUG / snag | "Get snug, together." | `caution` Snug Safety (daily check-in app) and Snugs (community platform) exist. | Short names are mostly taken as domains and handles. |
| **Pillowfort** | A blanket fort for movie night. | Childhood joy, very warm. | PILL-o-fort / pilou-fort | "Build the fort, press play." | `taken` Pillowfort is a known social network (closing Dec 2026, the name will still be remembered). | Same field (social), famous name. |
| **Afterglow** | The warm feeling after a good film. | Romantic, evening. | AF-ter-glow / afır-glou | "Stay for the afterglow." | `risky` An 18+ social chat app called Afterglow exists, plus several journals. | Adult association: bad for a family-friendly store listing. |
