# Session recap: cinema look and music options (card RC2, 21 Nov 2026, lane A2)

The owner picks one look and one music option through the Planner. The recap's rules (button after 2 h, once per
session, quiet start) don't depend on the pick and ship first.

## Looks (mock-ups, 800 × 450; the real one animates)
- **A · Velvet curtains** (`a-velvet-curtains.png`, recommended): red curtains open at the start and close at the end,
  a warm spotlight, gold serif type. It is closest to "cinema" and works with every theme.
- **B · Film reel credits** (`b-film-reel-credits.png`): sprocket strips, film grain and a projector flicker. The facts
  roll upward like end credits ("Starring Alex · Sam · Lee", "Most laughs: Sam 😂 ×87").
- **C · Marquee sign** (`c-marquee-sign.png`): a theatre marquee with chasing bulbs and each fact on the sign
  ("Now showing: our movie night").

## Music
All options start at about −18 dB with a 3 s fade-in, follow the room's sound switch and volume, and stop when the
recap ends.
1. **Our own soft pad, re-voiced** (recommended): Web Audio, no file and no licence. It is today's pad made quieter,
   slower and warmer, with a soft piano-like pluck.
2. **"Cinematic Piano BGM"** by Roskovair (itch.io), CC0: soft piano, recorded, no credit needed. A ~1 MB file, cut and
   looped.
3. **A CC0 ambient piano theme** by Yoiyami (OpenGameArt, "The Budding of Consciousness"), CC0: calmer and more
   dreamy.
4. **A Kevin MacLeod (incompetech) piece**, CC BY 4.0: wide choice, but it needs a credit line in
   `third-party-notices.txt`.

The licence of each file is checked on its own page before it is added. A CC0 file still gets a line in
`apps/web/public/third-party-notices.txt`.
