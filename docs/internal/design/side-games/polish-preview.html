Five Frames: new board

🎟️ Five Frames #12 · 🔥 3

Flip: each cell turns 0 → 90° in 110 ms, takes its colour at 90°, turns back in 110 ms; the next cell starts 80 ms later (≈ 540 ms for five). The tray changes after the last cell; a solved row hops (−6 px, 120 ms, 60 ms apart). An unknown word shakes the row.

Reduced motion: no turn, hop or shake: colours cross-fade in 150 ms and an unknown word gets a red outline.

Colours: right place #3fcf8e + ✓ (9.0:1), in the word #ffc65a + • (11.5:1), not in it #3a3458 (7.2:1). Cells 46 px, letters 22 px, keys 44 px tall with 17 px letters.
