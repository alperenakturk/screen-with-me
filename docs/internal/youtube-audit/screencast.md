# The screencast (2–3 minutes, on https://sidelit.app)

Recorded by B2 in a real Chrome on the live domain (computer, 1366 × 768 or larger), uploaded as an unlisted video, the
link pasted in the form. No voice needed; each scene shows for a few seconds. Never show a camera window over the player.

1. **Home → Create room** (5 s): the home page, press "Create room", type a name, enter.
2. **Search** (25 s): type "nature documentary", press Enter. Pause on the result list: the YouTube logo, "Results from
   YouTube · YouTube Terms of Service". Hover the logo (it links to youtube.com).
3. **Play** (20 s): press a result. The embedded YouTube player plays with its own controls; nothing of ours over it; our
   control column sits beside it. Pause and seek once.
4. **Shorts view and Discover** (30 s): open the Shorts view, the ♾ window, pick a category; the list with the logo line;
   "✨ More like this".
5. **Queue** (10 s): the queue rows with the YouTube titles and thumbnails, unmodified.
6. **Privacy policy** (20 s): open Privacy from the room's ⋯ More; scroll to "YouTube search": the YouTube API Services,
   the Google Privacy Policy link, how long answers are kept.
7. **Terms** (10 s): `terms.html`, the YouTube line ("By playing or searching YouTube videos in Sidelit you agree to…").
8. **Phone** (20 s, optional): the same search on a phone, the result list with the logo line.
