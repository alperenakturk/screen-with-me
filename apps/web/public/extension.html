Sidelit

Watch videos on any site with your friends, in sync.

Free No account Chrome · Edge · Brave · Opera

💻 The extension is for computers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera). On this phone or tablet you can still watch YouTube and video links together on the site, no extension needed: open Sidelit.

⬇ Download the extension

What does it do?

The video plays in everyone's own browser; the extension only carries tiny messages like "pause, go to 12:34".

🎬

Any website

Finds the video on the page by itself, even when the player is tucked inside another part of the page. Wrong one? Pick the right one with 🎯.

⏯️

Tight sync

Play, pause, seek, speed: when one person does it, it happens for everyone. Anyone who falls behind catches up.

🍿

The room over the film

Chat, emojis, voice chat and controls around the video, just like on the site. In fullscreen too.

🔒

Private & steady

Only works in the tab you joined a room with. Other speed extensions can't knock the room out of sync.

Why does Chrome say it can "read and change all your data on all websites"? A film can be on any site, so the extension has to be able to find the video there.
It does nothing while you are not in a room; in a room it only follows the film's play, pause, position and speed in the tab you opened from the room. What it can and cannot see →

How to use it

Your friends need the extension too (not for YouTube: that plays right on the site).

🏠

1. Create a room on the site

Create a room on Sidelit (or join with a friend's link).

🔗

2. Paste the video page

Paste the link of the page with the video and press "Play now". The tab goes to that page.

🍿

3. Watch together

The room (chat, emojis, controls) opens around the video right away, just like on the site. Friends join by pressing "Go to the film".

Tip: some pages are full of ads. If pop-ups or ads interrupt watching, you can install a free ad blocker such as uBlock Origin Lite. It is a separate extension, not part of Sidelit.

FAQ

How do I update it?

Download the new zip from this page, extract it into the same folder (overwrite the old files) and press ↻ on the Sidelit card on the extensions page, then reload the site's tab. ↻ only re-reads the folder the extension was loaded from: if you extracted the new zip somewhere else, press Remove on the old card and load the new folder with "Load unpacked". The site's "Extension" button shows a dot while a newer version is out.

The video isn't detected

Something (an ad, a pop-up) may be covering it: press 👁 Show page at the top, close it or start the video, then go back to room view. If it still isn't found, reload the page.

Does it work with subscription streaming services?

No, services with encrypted (DRM) video aren't supported.

Firefox or Safari?

The extension works only in Chromium-based browsers for now (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi). The website itself opens in Firefox and Safari too: rooms with YouTube or video files, chat, games and voice work without the extension.

Voice or camera doesn't connect

Some privacy settings block direct connections between browsers, and voice, cameras and screen sharing need them. Brave / Vivaldi: Settings → Privacy → "WebRTC IP handling policy" → Default. uBlock Origin: turn off "Prevent WebRTC from leaking local IP addresses". Opera: turn off the built-in VPN while you're in voice. The room tells you when this is the reason.

What data does it use?

Only in the tab you joined a room with: the state of the video on the page (playing or not, at which second). It doesn't collect browsing history. Details: privacy policy.

Sık sorulanlar

Nasıl güncellerim?

Bu sayfadan yeni zip'i indir, aynı klasörün içine çıkar (eskilerin üzerine yaz), eklentiler sayfasındaki Sidelit kartında ↻ düğmesine bas, sonra sitenin sekmesini yenile. ↻ sadece eklentinin yüklendiği klasörü yeniden okur: yeni zip'i başka bir klasöre çıkardıysan eski karttaki Kaldır'a bas ve yeni klasörü "Paketlenmemiş öğe yükle" ile seç. Yeni sürüm çıktığında sitedeki "Eklenti" düğmesinde bir nokta görünür.

Video algılanmıyor

Önünü bir reklam ya da pencere kapatıyor olabilir: üstteki 👁 Sayfayı göster'e bas, kapat ya da videoyu başlat, sonra oda görünümüne dön. Yine bulunmazsa sayfayı yenile.

Abonelikli dizi/film servislerinde çalışır mı?

Hayır, şifreli (DRM'li) video kullanan servisler desteklenmiyor.

Firefox ya da Safari?

Eklenti şimdilik sadece Chromium tabanlı tarayıcılarda çalışıyor (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi). Sitenin kendisi Firefox ve Safari'de de açılır: YouTube ya da video dosyası olan odalar, sohbet, oyunlar ve sesli sohbet eklentisiz çalışır.

Ses ya da kamera bağlanmıyor

Bazı gizlilik ayarları tarayıcılar arasındaki doğrudan bağlantıyı engeller; ses, kamera ve ekran paylaşımı buna ihtiyaç duyar. Brave / Vivaldi: Ayarlar → Gizlilik → "WebRTC IP handling policy" → Varsayılan. uBlock Origin: "WebRTC'nin yerel IP sızdırmasını önle" kapalı olsun. Opera: sesteyken yerleşik VPN'i kapat. Sebep buysa oda sana söyler.

Hangi bilgileri kullanıyor?

Sadece odaya katıldığın sekmede, sayfadaki videonun durumunu (oynuyor mu, kaçıncı saniyede). Gezinti geçmişini toplamaz. Ayrıntılar: gizlilik politikası.
