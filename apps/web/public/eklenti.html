What does it do?

The video plays in everyone's own browser; the extension only carries tiny messages like "pause, go to 12:34".

🎬

Any website

Finds the video on the page by itself, even when the player is inside an iframe.

⏯️

Tight sync

Play, pause, seek, speed: when one person does it, it happens for everyone. Whoever falls behind catches up.

😂

Reactions & messages

Emojis (▲▼ for funnier ones) and short messages over the video, in fullscreen too.

🔒

Private & steady

Only works in the tab you joined a room with. Other speed extensions can't knock the room out of sync.

How to use it

Your friends need the extension too (not for YouTube — that you watch right on the site).

🏠

1. Create a room on the site

Create a room on ScreenWithMe (or join with a friend's link).

🔗

2. Paste the video page

Paste the link of the page with the video and press "Play now". The tab goes to that page.

🍿

3. Watch together

The room (chat, emojis, controls) appears around the video right away, just like on the site. Friends press "Go to the film".

Tip: some pages are full of ads. If pop-ups or ads interrupt watching, you can install a free ad blocker such as uBlock Origin Lite. It is a separate extension, not part of ScreenWithMe.

FAQ

How do I update it?

Download the new zip from this page, extract it into the same folder (overwrite the old files) and press ↻ on the ScreenWithMe card on the extensions page.

The video isn't detected

Something (an ad, a pop-up) may be covering it: press 👁 Show page at the top, close it or start the video, then go back to room view. If it still isn't found, reload the page.

Does it work with Netflix / Disney+?

No, encrypted (DRM) services are not supported.

Firefox?

Not yet, we're working on it.

What data does it use?

Only in the tab you joined a room with: the state of the video on the page (playing or not, at which second). It doesn't collect browsing history. Details: privacy policy.

Nasıl kullanılır?

Arkadaşlarının da eklentiyi kurmuş olması gerekiyor (YouTube için gerekmez, onu sitede izlersiniz).

🏠

1. Sitede oda kur

ScreenWithMe'de oda kur (ya da arkadaşının linkiyle katıl).

🔗

2. Videonun sayfasını yapıştır

Videonun olduğu sayfanın linkini yapıştırıp "Şimdi aç"a bas. Sekme o sayfaya gider.

🍿

3. Birlikte izle

Oda (sohbet, emojiler, kontroller) videonun etrafında hemen açılır, sitedekinin aynısı. Arkadaşların "Filme git"e basar.

İpucu: bazı sayfalar çok reklamlıdır. Açılır pencereler ya da reklamlar izlemeyi bölerse uBlock Origin Lite gibi ücretsiz bir reklam engelleyici kurabilirsin. Ayrı bir eklentidir, ScreenWithMe'nin parçası değildir.
