Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Videos play in your own browser; our server only relays the small messages needed to keep everyone in the room in sync.

What we use

  • Name and avatar: what you pick when joining a room. Shown to the others in the room. Your browser remembers it per room (a change in one room never changes another room) and offers your last choice when you join a new room; on our server it is kept only together with the room (in its chat history and, if you are removed, in its ban list) and deleted with the room.
  • Room state: the address of the current video or page, playing/paused, position, speed and the queue.
  • Chat messages: the last 50 messages of a room, stored with the room so people who join later can see them.
  • Images in chat: pictures or GIFs you send in a room's chat (photos are resized to at most 1024 px in your browser first, which also removes hidden photo data such as location; hidden notes inside GIFs are removed too). Everyone in the room sees them and can save them. They are stored only with that room, at most the images of its last 50 messages (up to 12), and are deleted with the room.
  • Votes and room options: which queued videos got a 👍 (stored with your browser ID so a vote can be taken back; others only see the count), the host modes and a scheduled start time. Deleted with the room.
  • Session recap: how many messages, reactions and pins each person sent, how often they paused or skipped, and how long and which videos were watched (people are told apart by browser ID; only names and avatars appear in the recap). Only the person who asks sees the recap. The counters reset when a new session starts after a break of more than 4 hours. Recaps someone saves with "Save" (up to 20 in a saved room, 5 otherwise; at most 90 days) are stored with the room and deleted with it.
  • Your saved rooms list: the names and codes of the rooms you saved are kept only in your browser.
  • Side games: your progress in the games you play alone beside the video (Snack Bar) is kept only in your browser and never sent anywhere; the game only reads, on your screen, whether the room's video is playing and how many people are in the room or in voice.
  • Your microphone, camera and speaker choice, and camera layout: which devices to use for voice and video chat (your browser's device codes), whether to join muted or with the camera on, where you put each camera tile and whose cameras you hid are kept only in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • Saved room (if the host turns it on): the addresses of the last 30 videos played in the room, where you stopped and their pins are stored with the room, so a video continues where you left off when it is opened again. Deleted as soon as the host turns saving off.
  • Pinned moments: the second of the current video, an optional short note and the name/avatar of who pinned it. Visible to the room; reset when the video changes and deleted with the room.
  • Custom emojis: images or GIFs you add to a room (pictures are resized to 160 px, which also removes hidden photo data such as location; hidden notes inside GIFs are removed too). They are stored only with that room, everyone in the room can see and save them, and they are deleted with the room (at most 24 per room; the oldest goes first). You, or the host, can remove one at any time. If you choose "Also save to my collection", a copy is kept only in your browser (and the extension's storage on your computer); it is sent to a room only when you add it there yourself.
  • Browser ID: a random code created in your browser. Used so the room host keeps the host role after reloading, and so someone the host removed can't rejoin that room until the host lifts the ban. It is stored with the room on our server for this purpose and deleted with the room. The ID itself is never shown to other people. It is not an account.

All room data (state, chat, settings, saved videos) is deleted 24 hours after the last person leaves (30 days if the host saved the room).

What we don't use

  • No account, email, phone number or password is asked for.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking tools, and none of their cookies.
  • Video content never passes through our server; everyone opens the video themselves.
  • Data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Browser extension

  • The extension only works in the tab you joined a room with. It reads and sends nothing in other tabs.
  • In that tab it only uses the state of the video on the page (playing or not, at which second, speed).
  • When you open a page in the room, that page's address and title are shared with the room.
  • It does not collect browsing history.
  • On the film page the extension shows our own website (the room page) as a layer over the page; the room connection is the website's, exactly as on the site. The film site cannot read this layer.
  • Your website settings (name, avatar, theme, language, browser ID, saved rooms, your custom emoji collection) are also kept in the extension's own storage in your browser, so the layer on the film page uses the same settings. Nothing extra is sent anywhere.
  • The "access all sites" permission is needed so videos can be found on any site (including inside iframes).
  • While you watch in the room view, a tab that the video page opens to another site right after you click on it (usually an ad pop-up) is closed and the film tab is brought back; a small note lets you reopen it. Only the address of that tab is used, only on your computer, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn this off in the room settings.
  • Voice chat on film pages runs in an invisible page of the extension ("offscreen" permission), so the microphone permission is asked once for Sidelit and not for the film site. The film site cannot access your microphone through us.

Voice chat

  • Your microphone is used only after you press "Join voice"; the browser asks for permission and you can revoke it any time in its settings. "Leave" turns the microphone off.
  • Your voice goes directly (peer-to-peer, always encrypted) to the others who joined voice. Our server never hears it; it only passes the connection messages between people who joined voice. It is not recorded or stored.
  • Because the connection is direct, everyone who is in voice with you (including people who join voice later) can technically see your public IP address (roughly your city and internet provider, not your home address). You are told this before you join. People who haven't joined voice can't see it. To set up these direct connections your browser asks Cloudflare's STUN server for your public address. During the room our server only keeps who is in voice, whether their microphone and camera are on, and who is streaming or watching.
  • Screen sharing / streams: your screen, window or tab is only captured after you press "Share your screen" and pick what to share in the browser's own window; your camera is added to the corner of the picture only if you ticked "Show my camera in the corner too". It goes peer-to-peer (encrypted) only to the people who press "Watch stream". As in voice, this works in both directions: the person streaming and the people watching can see each other's public IP address (both are told before they start). Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Your camera is used only after you press 📷 while in voice, and only goes (peer-to-peer, encrypted) to the others in voice. It is not recorded or stored; pressing 📷 again or leaving voice turns it off.
  • Others in the room could record their own screen or speakers; we can't prevent that. Only talk with people you trust.
  • TV: a TV you connect with its 6-digit code joins the room as you (your name and avatar). To let it reconnect, the room keeps a random link token with your name, avatar and browser id for at most 12 hours after its last use; "Disconnect the TV" deletes it. The TV never uses a microphone; it only plays the voices of the others in voice (so, like everyone in voice, they and the TV can see each other's IP address). The code itself is not stored and is valid for 10 minutes.

Games (PlayWithMe)

  • Games are started inside a watch room. A game keeps the board, the score, the players' names and avatars and their browser IDs (so a player who reloads keeps their seat and points; IDs are never shown to others). Emoji reactions are not stored.
  • Draw & Guess: guesses and the short messages typed during a game are passed on to the other players and not stored. The drawing exists only while the round is played (in the game server's memory) and is gone when the round ends.
  • A game's data is deleted 24 hours after the last player leaves; the room only remembers which game is open (and who opened it) until the game is ended.

Infrastructure

The site and server run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service, and keeps short technical logs (time, address requested, errors) for a few days. To stop abuse (for example someone opening rooms in a loop), our server counts new connections and custom emoji uploads per IP address; it only uses a shortened, scrambled form of the address, in memory, and never stores it or shows it to anyone. See the Cloudflare privacy policy. YouTube videos open in YouTube's own player and Google's privacy policy applies.

YouTube search

The search in the room's link box uses the YouTube API Services. When you search, our server sends only the words you typed (and the page language) to YouTube and shows you the videos it finds; it does not send your name, your room or anything that identifies you, and you never sign in to YouTube. To save the daily search limit, our server keeps the results for the same words for up to 24 hours (by the words, not by person) and counts searches per IP address in the same shortened, scrambled form described above. By using the search you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy applies to what YouTube receives. The same applies to "🎲 Discover" and "✨ More like this" in the Shorts view: Discover asks YouTube for the most watched Shorts of a category (no words of yours at all) and keeps one shared answer per category, language and day; More like this sends only the id of the Short that is playing.

YouTube videos

A YouTube video in a room plays in YouTube's own official embedded player in its privacy-enhanced mode, loaded from youtube-nocookie.com in your browser. YouTube's player code is loaded only once a YouTube video is about to play in your room, never before. YouTube may receive your IP address, and YouTube says this mode stores no information in your browser until you play the video; once it plays, the Google privacy policy applies. Video pictures in the queue and search results come from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com).

TikTok videos

A TikTok link in a room plays in TikTok's own official embedded player, loaded from tiktok.com in your browser; TikTok may receive your IP address and set its own cookies, as on any site that shows a TikTok video, and the TikTok privacy policy applies to that. To show the video's title in the queue, our server asks TikTok's public oEmbed service about the link (nothing about you is sent). When you paste a short TikTok share link (vm.tiktok.com/…), our server opens it once to find the video it points to and keeps that answer for a week (by the link, not by person).

Vimeo videos

A Vimeo link in a room plays in Vimeo's own embedded player, loaded from player.vimeo.com in your browser; we open it in Vimeo's "do not track" mode (dnt=1), but Vimeo still receives your IP address like on any site that shows a Vimeo video, and the Vimeo privacy policy applies to that. To show the video's title in the queue, our server asks Vimeo's public oEmbed service about the link (nothing about you is sent).

Content

Sidelit does not host or relay any video. It only syncs the players everyone opens themselves. Only use content you have the right to watch.
